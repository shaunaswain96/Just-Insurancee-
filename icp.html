What Is
An ICP?

An HOA takes a dozen scattered responsibilities — landscaping, security, maintenance, reserves — and bundles them into one flat package so homeowners never have to think about who handles what. An ICP (Insurance Coverage Package) does the same thing for insurance.

Instead of a General Liability policy from one agent, Builders Risk from another, and a personal umbrella you never got around to, you get one pre-built package matched to exactly what you do — builder, developer, or investor — with every coverage a person in your position actually needs, none of the ones you don't, and one advisor accountable for the whole thing.

You stop shopping for insurance. You just tell me which ICP fits your business, and I build, bind, and manage it.

The Packages

Four ICPs. Built For How You Actually Work.

Every line item below is a real coverage, chosen for what actually causes claims in this line of work — not a generic checklist.

ICP · 01

The Builder ICP

For active general contractors, remodelers & trade contractors
Core Coverages
  • General LiabilityProject and completed-operations coverage that meets what every GC contract and permit requires.
  • Workers' CompStatutory coverage for crew injuries on the job site — the #1 thing that shuts down a job if it's missing.
  • Commercial AutoTrucks, trailers, and hauled equipment moving between sites and suppliers.
  • Builders RiskThe structure itself while under construction — fire, theft, wind, and vandalism before it's insurable as a finished property.
  • Tools & EquipmentInland marine floater for tools and equipment on-site, in transit, or in storage — not covered by GL or auto.
  • UmbrellaExtra limit above GL, auto, and workers' comp for the one bad claim that exceeds your primary limits.
Add When It Applies
  • Contractor's Pollution Liability — mold, asbestos, fuel/chemical exposure from site work
  • License & Permit Surety Bonds — required to bid or pull permits in most municipalities
  • Subcontractor COI Tracking — I verify every sub carries their own coverage and names you as additional insured, so their gap never becomes your claim
  • Cyber Liability — protects bid data, client info, and payment/ACH details
  • Employment Practices Liability — once you're hiring beyond a small core crew

Built around what actually causes claims on a job site: a worker injury, a sub with no coverage, a fire before the structure is finished, or a truck accident between sites.

ICP · 02

The Developer ICP

For land acquisition, entitlement, and multi-unit or spec builds
Core Coverages
  • Builders Risk (COC)Course-of-construction form built to cover multiple concurrent projects and phases, not just one address.
  • Owner's Protective LiabilityCovers you as the developer for claims arising from your contractors' work — or a full OCIP wrap-up on larger projects.
  • Umbrella / ExcessHigher limits sized to project value — developer exposure runs well past standard GC limits.
  • Environmental LiabilitySite contamination and remediation costs — the single most common six-figure surprise in ground-up development.
  • Delay in CompletionSoft-cost coverage for lost financing costs, interest, and taxes if a covered loss pushes back your completion date.
  • FloodSite and structure flood coverage — non-negotiable for ground-up work in Houston's flood zones.
Add When It Applies
  • Subcontractor Default Insurance — protects the project if a major sub fails to perform or goes under mid-build
  • Directors & Officers — for the development LLC and its members/partners
  • Cyber Liability with wire-fraud coverage — protects earnest money and closing funds from the exact scam real estate deals attract
  • Errors & Omissions — if you also advise, broker, or sell as part of the development
  • Key Person / Buy-Sell Life Insurance — funds a partner buyout if a principal dies mid-project

Built for the exposures that scale with project size: contamination, a failed sub, a delayed close, and wire fraud — not the smaller claims a single-job builder worries about.

ICP · 03

The Investor ICP

For rental portfolios, flips, and short-term rentals
Core Coverages
  • Landlord PropertyPer-property or blanket/portfolio policy covering every unit under one schedule instead of a dozen separate policies.
  • Loss of RentsReplaces rental income while a covered loss makes a unit uninhabitable.
  • Premises LiabilityTenant or visitor injury on the property — the most common landlord claim by volume.
  • Umbrella / ExcessCritical once you hold more than one property — liability exposure compounds across the whole portfolio.
  • FloodProperty-level flood coverage for every unit in a flood zone, tracked per address.
  • Vacant Property / Reno RiskGap coverage for flips or units between tenants, when a standard landlord policy stops applying.
Add When It Applies
  • Equipment Breakdown — HVAC, appliances, and mechanical systems in multi-family units
  • Business Personal Property — furnished units and short-term rental furnishings/electronics
  • Ordinance or Law Coverage — code-upgrade costs on older properties after a covered loss
  • Employment Practices Liability — once you employ property managers or maintenance staff
  • Cyber Liability — tenant application data and short-term rental platform payment info

Built around the two things that actually hurt a landlord: a liability claim that outruns a single-property limit, and lost rent while a unit sits unrentable.

ICP · 04 · The Flagship

The All-In ICP

For people who build, develop, and hold rentals at the same time
What's Bundled
  • Every Core CoverageAll Builder, Developer, and Investor core coverages that apply to your actual business, combined under one file.
  • One Renewal CalendarEvery policy aligned to a single review cycle instead of six different renewal dates from six different carriers.
  • One Point of ContactMe. For every claim, every certificate request, every new project that needs a quote fast.
  • Priority Claims HandlingDirect claims guidance across every policy in the package — you're never bounced between carriers.
Why It Exists

Most people in this business don't fit neatly into one box — a builder who also holds a rental or two, a developer who general-contracts their own projects. The All-In ICP is the actual HOA-style promise: you stop managing insurance entirely. One conversation a year, one advisor accountable for the whole portfolio, and every gap between "builder," "developer," and "investor" coverage — the exact seams where people usually get burned — closed on purpose instead of by accident.
